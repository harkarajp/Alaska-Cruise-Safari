Seattle, WA
Departs 4:00 PM
64°F / 18°C Partly Sunny & Mild

✨ Port Lore & Facts
The Emerald City! Known for its tech giants, coffee culture, and stunning views of Mount Rainier. We sail straight out of Pier 66.
📍 Top Spots to See
  • Space Needle & Chihuly Gardens: Unmissable panoramic views right before boarding.
  • Pike Place Market: Catching the famous flying fish throwing action.
🧥

The Encore boasts a massive 1,100-foot electric go-kart race track overhanging the sea, alongside incredible dining lounges perfect for a massive cousin hangout.

Tuesday, June 2
Juneau, Alaska
2:30 PM – 10:00 PM
54°F / 12°C Showers Likely
🌧️
✨ Port Lore & Facts
Alaska's isolated capital city. It cannot be reached by roads—only by air or water! It sits right at the base of the massive Juneau Icefield.
📍 Top Spots to See
  • Mendenhall Glacier: Stand feet away from a stunning massive blue ice flow.
  • Gold Creek Salmon Bake: Historic gold-rush style dining experience out in the rainforest.
☔
Outfit Check: Rainwear day! Waterproof outer shell jacket, waterproof boots/shoes, and warm layers underneath. Juneau is notoriously wet, so leave the nice canvas shoes on the ship.
Wednesday, June 3
Skagway, Alaska
6:00 AM – 8:15 PM
57°F / 14°C Partly Cloudy & Crisp
⛅
✨ Port Lore & Facts
The heart of the 1898 Klondike Gold Rush. The entire downtown district looks like a preserved 19th-century wild-west frontier outpost.
📍 Top Spots to See
  • White Pass & Yukon Route Railroad: Ride vintage train coaches climbing up rugged mountain cliffs.
  • Klondike Highway Viewpoints: Stunning turquoise alpine lakes and sweeping valleys.
🧣
Outfit Check: True modular layering. A warm beanie, thermal inner-shirt, fleece sweater, and windbreaker. The mountain passes on the train ride get significantly colder!
Thursday, June 4
Glacier Bay National Park
Scenic Cruising
48°F / 9°C Chilly Glacial Winds
❄️
✨ Port Lore & Facts
A UNESCO World Heritage site containing spectacular tidewater glaciers. The ship stays quiet to listen for "white thunder"—the sound of massive icebergs calving into the sea.
📍 Top Spots to See
  • Margerie Glacier: The main spectacular display point where the ship rotates 360 degrees.
  • Wildlife Spotting: Watch the waters closely for sea otters, harbor seals, and humpback whales.
🥶
Outfit Check: Coldest day of the trip! Bring out your heavy winter jacket, gloves, scarf, and double up on socks if you plan to stand out on the open observation decks.
Friday, June 5
Ketchikan (Ward Cove), AK
7:00 AM – 1:15 PM
56°F / 13°C Misty & Overcast
☁️
✨ Port Lore & Facts
The Salmon Capital of the World and home to the world's largest collection of standing Native American totem poles. The Encore docks at historic Ward Cove.
📍 Top Spots to See
  • Creek Street: Historic boardwalk built on stilts over a rushing salmon stream.
  • Totem Bight State Park: Beautiful hand-carved totems set against the forest.
🧥
Outfit Check: Comfortable walking layers with a water-resistant jacket. Ketchikan is a rainforest environment, so carrying a compact umbrella is a smart backup plan.
Saturday, June 6
Victoria, BC, Canada
8:00 PM – 11:59 PM
60°F / 16°C Clear & Cool Evening
🌙
✨ Port Lore & Facts
Located on Vancouver Island, Victoria is incredibly famous for its classic British colonial architecture, gorgeous manicured flower gardens, and old-world charm.
📍 Top Spots to See
  • The Inner Harbour: The lit-up night silhouette of the Parliament Buildings is magical.
  • Empress Hotel: A breathtaking historic grand hotel right on the harbor water.
✨
Outfit Check: Elegant evening casual! Great night for a smart blazer or a beautiful warm bright dupatta/shawl over a jacket as we walk the illuminated city streets.
